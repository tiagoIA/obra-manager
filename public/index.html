A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Who is CrewNelo for?

CrewNelo is being developed for electrical, HVAC and construction service teams that need a practical way to organize jobs, crew schedules and field records.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The workspace is browser-based with a layout for phones and computers. Open it in your browser; the demonstration also works on mobile.

Does the demo connect to a real company?

No. It uses fictional people, jobs and materials, and keeps changes only for this visit. It never reads or changes live company data. Reset it anytime.

Can my company sign up today?

Self-service company registration is coming soon. We’re preparing company workspaces and access controls before opening registration. You can explore the demo now. Existing invited users can log in.

What about pricing and payments?

Commercial plans and subscriptions have not launched. The demo is free to explore. The workspace tracks purchases and expenses; customer invoicing and payment collection are not available.

Is customer management available?

A dedicated customer directory and customer-to-job history are planned. Current features focus on projects, units, tasks, scheduling, field records, time, materials and costs.
